Common questions

Why does it say price on enquiry instead of a price?
Because no prices were invented for this demo. The salon confirms the price in writing after you pick a service.

Do I have to leave this page to book?
No, not in this demo. Service selection and booking sit on the same page.

Is this Union Street Salon's official website?
No. This is a demo concept built by KezTech Solution to show how clearer service and price steps could work. It is not the official site for Union Street Salon.
